Our Brands

Four Members of One Luxury Family.

Each brand stands on its own — its own name, team, and clientele. Together, they follow a woman from becoming, to dressing, to mothering, to celebrating.

Aries Bloom Inc. brings beauty, fashion, wellness, and celebration together under one roof — not as four competing businesses, but as four specialists serving the same woman at different points in her life. Explore each brand below to see what makes it distinct, and how it connects to the rest of the family.

01 · Beauty & Self-Expression

BibisGlambar

BibisGlambar began the way most beautiful things do — quietly, with a steady hand and a labor of love. Long before it grew into a full studio, it was a single instinct: that beauty work is really about attention — about seeing someone fully.

◈ Mission

To help every woman feel confident, seen, and beautiful — never by hiding who she is, but by drawing her natural beauty forward.

✦ Signature Offering

Bridal glam trials and day-of styling — the service that started it all, still perfected one client at a time.

◇ Who We Serve

Brides, professionals, and any woman seeking premium beauty services for milestone moments or elevated everyday glam.

◱ What We Offer

Bridal hair & makeup trials, day-of styling, editorial and event glam, and personalized beauty consultations.

→ Looking Ahead

A flagship studio space and a curated line of signature beauty products, both currently in development.

Visit BibisGlambar
02 · Fashion & Couture

Sasy Couture

The women BibisGlambar styled kept asking the same question: where do I find a dress that actually fits me? Sasy Couture was built to answer it — a luxury fashion house dedicated to timeless elegance and exceptional craftsmanship.

◈ Mission

To prove that fashion can tell a story and empower the woman wearing it, one handcrafted garment at a time.

✦ Signature Offering

Made-to-order bridal and occasion couture, fitted over multiple sessions to the body alone.

◇ Who We Serve

Women seeking made-to-order gowns and occasionwear built for their exact measurements, never a size chart.

◱ What We Offer

Bridal couture, custom occasionwear, multi-session fittings, and hand-finished embellishment work.

→ Looking Ahead

A made-to-order ready-to-wear collection, extending Sasy Couture's craftsmanship beyond fully custom pieces.

Visit Sasy Couture
03 · Motherhood & Wellness

Baddie Mums Club

As beauty and couture clients became mothers, a new question emerged — where did the rest of me go? Baddie Mums Club exists for exactly that woman: a wellness and confidence community built around beauty, fitness, self-care, and connection.

◈ Mission

To help mothers rediscover themselves, without asking them to set their identity aside.

✦ Signature Offering

Small-group wellness circles pairing movement, mindset, and honest conversation.

◇ Who We Serve

Mothers at every stage — from new motherhood to raising teenagers — seeking community and a renewed sense of self.

◱ What We Offer

Small-group wellness circles, styling sessions, fitness and mindset programming, and community events.

→ Looking Ahead

Regional in-person chapters, expanding the community beyond its current digital-first footprint.

Visit Baddie Mums Club
04 · Celebration & Events

BB Soirée & Events

The milestones our clients were already planning around us — baby showers, bridal moments, birthdays that mattered — became a brand of their own. BB Soirée & Events is a luxury event house dedicated to the art of celebration.

◈ Mission

To make sure life's most meaningful moments are planned and honored the way they deserve to be.

✦ Signature Offering

Full-service celebration planning — design, decor, and day-of management in one hand.

◇ Who We Serve

Clients planning bridal showers, baby showers, milestone birthdays, and other life-marking celebrations.

◱ What We Offer

Full-service event design, decor styling, vendor coordination, and day-of event management.

→ Looking Ahead

A signature rental collection of decor and staging pieces, exclusive to Aries Bloom clients.

Visit BB Soirée & Events
