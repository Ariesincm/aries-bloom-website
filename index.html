About Aries Bloom Inc.

One House. Four Brands. One Purpose.

Aries Bloom Inc. is the parent holding company behind four independently operated, purpose-built brands: BibisGlambar, Sasy Couture, Baddie Mums Club, and BB Soirée & Events. Each brand serves its own market with its own team, expertise, and identity — yet all four operate under one shared standard of excellence and one unified vision for what a modern lifestyle company should be.

Rather than competing for attention, our brands are designed to complement one another. BibisGlambar leads in beauty and self-expression. Sasy Couture leads in fashion and craftsmanship. Baddie Mums Club leads in wellness and community. BB Soirée & Events leads in celebration and experience design. Together, they cover the full arc of a woman's life — and Aries Bloom Inc. is the structure that keeps that arc coherent, consistent, and growing.

This is what distinguishes a holding company built with intention from a simple portfolio of businesses: every brand we bring into the Aries Bloom family is chosen because it strengthens the whole, not because it fills a gap. That discipline is our operating philosophy, and it is the foundation every one of our brands is built on.

Four Distinct Brands. One Extraordinary Vision.

Beauty & Self-Expression

BibisGlambar

BibisGlambar is a luxury beauty destination where artistry meets elegance. Known for its signature Timeless Soft Glam, the brand specializes in premium makeup artistry, bridal beauty, hairstyling, and personalized beauty experiences that enhance each client's natural features rather than conceal them.

Whether preparing for a wedding, milestone celebration, editorial shoot, or special occasion, every service is thoughtfully tailored to help women feel confident, radiant, and effortlessly beautiful. Every appointment reflects BibisGlambar's commitment to luxury, precision, and timeless elegance.

Fashion & Couture

Sasy Couture

Sasy Couture is a luxury fashion house dedicated to timeless elegance and exceptional craftsmanship. The brand specializes in custom couture, made-to-order gowns, and tailored occasionwear, designed for women who want a garment built around their body rather than a size chart.

Whether preparing for a wedding, a red-carpet moment, or a milestone event, every piece is hand-finished with meticulous attention to fit, fabric, and detail. Every fitting reflects Sasy Couture's commitment to individuality, femininity, and enduring style.

Motherhood & Wellness

Baddie Mums Club

Baddie Mums Club is a wellness and confidence community created for mothers who want to feel like themselves again. The brand offers wellness circles, styling sessions, and self-care experiences designed for women navigating motherhood at every stage.

Whether returning to a fitness routine, reconnecting with personal style, or simply finding a room that understands the weight of motherhood, every gathering is built to restore confidence and connection. Every session reflects Baddie Mums Club's commitment to community, care, and rediscovering who you are beyond "mom."

Celebration & Events

BB Soirée & Events

BB Soirée & Events is a luxury event planning and design house dedicated to unforgettable celebrations. The brand specializes in bridal showers, baby showers, milestone birthdays, and full-service event design for clients who want every detail considered.

Whether planning an intimate gathering or a large-scale celebration, every event is styled with the same restraint and elegance found throughout the Aries Bloom family. Every occasion reflects BB Soirée & Events' commitment to thoughtful design, flawless execution, and moments worth remembering.

Why Choose Aries Bloom

The Aries Bloom Difference

Every brand under the Aries Bloom name is built on the same foundation — four commitments that shape every service, every garment, every gathering, and every experience we create.

Excellence

We hold every detail — from a single hair strand to a wedding-day timeline — to a standard that never asks "is this good enough," only "is this our best." Excellence is not a claim we make; it is a discipline we practice.

Creativity

Innovation and thoughtful design guide everything we build. We treat beauty, fashion, wellness, and celebration as creative disciplines, never formulas — because every person we serve deserves something considered, original, and truly their own.

Empowerment

Every brand under the Aries Bloom name exists to help someone feel more like themselves, not less. Confidence is not something we sell — it is the outcome we design for, in every chair, fitting room, wellness circle, and event we create.

Meaningful Experiences

We are not in the business of transactions. A hairstyle, a gown, a wellness gathering, a celebration — these become memories, and memories are what we are truly building. Every Aries Bloom brand is committed to creating moments people carry with them long after they end.
