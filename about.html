Who We Are

One Vision. Four Distinct Brands.

Aries Bloom Inc. is the parent holding company behind BibisGlambar, Sasy Couture, Baddie Mums Club, and BB Soirée & Events — four brands, each with its own name, team, and clientele, built on one shared standard of care.

We exist because beauty, fashion, wellness, and celebration were never meant to be separate industries. They are one continuous experience in a woman's life, and Aries Bloom Inc. is the structure that keeps that experience coherent as it grows.

Leadership Philosophy

How We Operate as One Company

Aries Bloom Inc. is structured deliberately as a holding company — not a single business wearing four different names.

◈

Independent by Design

Each brand is led by its own specialists, sets its own creative direction, and answers to its own clientele — no shared branding, only a shared standard.

◈

Centralized Standards

Operations, brand governance, and quality control are coordinated centrally, so every brand scales without ever leaving quality to chance.

◈

One Ecosystem

Beauty, fashion, wellness, and celebration — four brands, each solving a distinct need, together following a woman through the fuller arc of her life.

Looking Ahead

The Future We're Building

Aries Bloom Inc. is still, at heart, a growing family. As new seasons of women's lives reveal new needs, we expect the house to keep growing with them — new services, new locations, and, in time, new brands added with the same discipline that built the first four.

Innovation at Aries Bloom Inc. means investing in the craft behind every brand — new techniques, new formats, and new ways of delivering the same standard of care at a larger scale. Expansion is deliberate: every new market, every new brand, is added only when it strengthens the whole company.

What won't change, no matter how large the house grows, is the standard beneath all of it: excellence, creativity, and a genuine belief in the people we serve.
