About Aries Bloom Inc.

Where Vision Became a Legacy

Aries Bloom Inc. is the story of one woman's belief that beauty, fashion, wellness, and celebration were never meant to be separate industries. They were always meant to be one continuous act of care.

Our Story

One Vision. Four Distinct Brands.

Aries Bloom Inc. is more than a company — it is a home. A home for beauty, for fashion, for wellness, and for the unforgettable moments that mark a life well lived. Beneath one name, four brands built with care and conviction come together: BibisGlambar, Sasy Couture, Baddie Mums Club, and BB Soirée & Events.

Each brand carries its own voice, its own craft, its own community — yet all four are guided by a single purpose: to help people see themselves clearly, wear their confidence openly, and celebrate the milestones that deserve to be remembered.

Leadership Philosophy

How We Operate as One Company

Aries Bloom Inc. is structured deliberately as a holding company, not a single business wearing four different names. Each brand — BibisGlambar, Sasy Couture, Baddie Mums Club, and BB Soirée & Events — is led by its own specialists, sets its own creative direction, and answers to its own clientele. What unites them is not shared branding, but a shared standard: the same commitment to craftsmanship, the same discipline around client care, and the same long-term thinking about what each brand is building toward.

This structure is intentional. Centralizing operations, brand governance, and quality standards allows every brand to focus entirely on its craft, while Aries Bloom Inc. ensures that craft is supported by real infrastructure — not left to chance as each brand scales.

We think of our four brands as one ecosystem rather than four separate ventures. BibisGlambar and Sasy Couture serve the moments a woman wants to look and feel her best. Baddie Mums Club supports her through one of life's most demanding transitions. BB Soirée & Events exists for the milestones worth marking. Individually, each brand solves a specific need. Together, under one holding company, they follow a woman through the fuller arc of her life — which is precisely the outcome Aries Bloom Inc. was built to deliver.
